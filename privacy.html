Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

WuYunLife Tools is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel 五蘊生活 (@wuyunlife) to manage that owner's own account. It is not offered to the public.

  • Data accessed: with the owner's consent via Google OAuth, the tools read the owner's own YouTube channel data, YouTube Analytics reports and AdSense reports, and upload or update the owner's own videos.
  • Storage: data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any third party.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
